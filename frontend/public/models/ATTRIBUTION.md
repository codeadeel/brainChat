# 3D asset attribution

- **head.glb / head-color.jpg / head-normal.jpg** — Lee Perry-Smith head scan,
  Infinite-Realities. Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**.
  Source: the Three.js examples (`models/gltf/LeePerrySmith/`).
- **brain.glb** — human brain with sculpted gyri, cerebellum and brainstem,
  authored by Innerscene Corp. Dedicated to the **public domain (CC0 1.0)**.
  Source: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/brain-10d8f88f

These are generic anatomical assets. They are not reconstructed from the
ds005777 participants' MRI. Optode positions are projected onto the generic
scalp from the dataset's recorded 2D coordinates.
